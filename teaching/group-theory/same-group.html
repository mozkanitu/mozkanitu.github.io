Theory of Groups in Physics, Week 1

Same group, different clothes

Compose permutations, then match the triangle’s symmetries with them. Two descriptions are the same group when their products agree.

Three numbered beads sit in three slots. A permutation moves the bead in slot i to slot σ(i), so (123) sends slot 1 to slot 2, slot 2 to slot 3 and slot 3 to slot 1. A product is read from right to left: in (12)(23), the factor (23) acts first.
