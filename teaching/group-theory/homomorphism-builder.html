Homomorphism builder

Choose where a generator goes, follow where everything else has to go, and see whether the map respects the group operation.

A homomorphism from Zm is fixed by one choice: where 1 goes. If 1 ↦ a, then k = 1 + 1 + ⋯ + 1 has to go to a + a + ⋯ + a = k · a. Choose the two groups and the image of 1, then check that the map is well defined.
