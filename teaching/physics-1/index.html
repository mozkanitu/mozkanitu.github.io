Physics I: Mechanics

FIZ 101, first year, Fall 2026

A first course in mechanics: vectors and motion, Newton's laws, work and energy, momentum and collisions, rotation, gravitation and oscillations.

Each topic has a reading, and most come with a short quiz and solutions. Videos and interactive simulations are added where they help.

Main text
H. D. Young and R. A. Freedman, University Physics, Volume I
Also useful
Giancoli; Halliday, Resnick and Walker; Serway and Jewett
Assessment
Quizzes 20%, midterm 40%, final exam 40%
