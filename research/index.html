Gravity, supersymmetry and quantum field theory

Carrollian physics and supersymmetry

Carroll symmetry is what relativity turns into when the speed of light goes to zero. I study Carroll contractions of supersymmetry algebras, including magnetic Carroll superalgebras, and construct the field theories that realize them, from Galileon-type models to links with SYK-like quantum mechanics.

Selected papers

    Off-shell supergravity

    Using superconformal tensor calculus, I construct off-shell supergravity theories and their higher-derivative invariants, particularly in five and six dimensions, together with their matter couplings.

    Selected papers

      Three-dimensional gravity and holography

      Massive and exotic gravity models in three dimensions and their Chern–Simons-like formulations, and non-relativistic (super)gravity theories built on extended Bargmann, Lifshitz, Schrödinger and Newton–Hooke algebras.

      Selected papers

        AI for theoretical physics

        How AI can be incorporated into theoretical-physics research without giving up control of the underlying calculations and scientific reasoning. I am developing an AI-assisted environment for supergravity calculations that combines symbolic computation, code, and step-by-step analytical work.
